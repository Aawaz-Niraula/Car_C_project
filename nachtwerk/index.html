    Lichtfaden

    Scroll to drive

    02

    One line of light, eleven metres long.

    Side-emitting optical fibre, laid by hand into a channel cut through the doors, sills and rear haunches. It shares a circuit with the headlamps and is the only ornament on the car.

    03

    Six cylinders behind the rear axle.

    A naturally aspirated 4.0-litre flat-six on individual throttle bodies, revving to 9,000 rpm, through a six-speed manual.

    • EngineFlat-six, 3,996 cc
    • Output525 PS at 8,750 rpm
    • DriveRear wheels, manual
    04

    Set up on wet concrete.

    The chassis was tuned on this floor, hosed down at night so the limit arrives slowly. Forged magnesium centre-lock wheels, 20 inches front and 21 rear, over carbon-ceramic discs.
