# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Stack

static HTML/CSS/JS, single page served as static files (existing build in `nachtwerk/`). Canvas frame-sequence engine is plain JS; no framework.

## Users

Primary: prospective web-design clients reviewing the owner's portfolio, deciding whether to commission a high-end (USD 10-20k) marketing site. They open it on a MacBook (ProMotion) or a phone, usually from a link, and judge within the first viewport whether the craft is studio-grade.

Secondary (in-world): the imagined buyer of the concept car, used to demonstrate that every control on the page really works.

## Product Purpose

A portfolio concept: a launch site for an invented coachbuilt car, built from the owner's 15-second AI-generated film of a graphite 911-style coupé with amber light lines driving through a wet, dark depot. Success: a client leaves believing the owner can ship an Awwwards-level, 120 fps scroll experience that works on mobile, and gets in touch.

## Positioning

The site proves craft through the film itself: scroll-scrubbed footage at sub-frame smoothness, sound that follows the scroll, and a page that does not look like the scroll-video template every AI tool produces.

## Constraints and commitments

- Brand: invented name only (currently "Nachtwerk", model "N/01 Lichtfaden"). No Porsche name or marks in copy; the crest visible in the footage is the owner's source material.
- Figures: the owner chose to keep invented specification figures (anti-slop R-37 override, recorded 2026-11-23). Present them as concept figures.
- Designer credit and contact: owner's name and email not yet supplied; use clear placeholders.
- Source assets: one 1280x720, 24 fps, 15 s video with audio; graded frames, stills and audio already extracted in `nachtwerk/`.
- Performance: zero perceptible lag, 120 Hz on ProMotion desktop, smooth on phones.
